# AI Attitude — Golden Slice Comparison and Decision

> Canonical Application Record · 2026-09-11

## Comparison result

### Legacy

The legacy experience used Room/Card/border-led structures. Uniform panels and persistent application chrome made it read more like a document or application UI than a lecture canvas.

### Direction A — AX DNA Direct

Strengths: warm amber identity, serif-led messages, soft-mask images, layered depth, low visual noise, and a reflective Closing. It is the closest of the three to the quiet exhibition quality established by AX Lecture.

Risk: repeating amber plus a large image on every slide can flatten the latter-half rhythm.

### Direction B — AI Attitude Native

Strengths: judgment, verification, and record-keeping are immediately legible through navy/teal, mono metadata, and evidence cues.

Risk: accumulated grid, mono typography, and brackets read as a tech demo or HUD; brackets can become decoration rather than meaning.

## Golden decision

> **Direction A = AI Attitude Web Deck 60 Golden Base**

Direction B is not adopted as a Skin. It is a limited source for evidence language only in Compare, Evidence, Verification, Record, Review, and Decision Point slides.

Allowed: a small evidence marker, micro label, thin verification line, limited mono metadata, and a subtle coordinate cue.

Prohibited: global grid, global mono typography, HUD chrome, repeated decorative brackets, and Direction B's complete visual language.

## Golden design rule

> **AI Attitude는 따뜻하고 절제된 성찰의 공간을 기본으로 하고, 판단이 필요한 순간에만 차가운 검증 언어를 짧게 삽입한다.**

## Application rule for the 19-slide polish

Direction A remains the base for warm amber, large serif statements, soft image masks, layered depth, restrained chrome, and the Closing callback. Rhythm changes by slide type rather than by changing the content: image-led concepts, typography statements, flows, evidence, comparison, record/review, key messages, and recaps must not repeat one composition for three or more slides. Mono evidence markers are deliberately limited to four semantic turns: Decision Point, core Evidence, Compare, and Record; Verification and Review retain only a thin line.

The approved Sprint 6 screenshots in `audit-assets/sprint6/` are Golden-decision evidence. They remain uncommitted until the full Golden implementation and QA are ready for one reviewed commit.
